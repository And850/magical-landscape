1. **0:00–0:20 — Purpose:** “Draw a cause. Discover a consequence. Magical Landscape is a persistent fantasy world you shape through painting.”
2. **0:20–0:45 — Initial geography:** paint sky, ground, mountains, a river, vegetation, rocks, and a structure. Show labeled brushes and the relative composition.
3. **0:45–1:05 — Bring it to life:** start actual local generation. Show progress; cut the waiting period with an explicit caption identifying that the wait was shortened. Show the resulting image.
4. **1:05–1:35 — Growth + Time:** paint the structure and surrounding ground. Optional text: “ancient roots reclaim this tower.” Apply magic and show the real result.
5. **1:35–2:00 — Water:** paint across a low area. Optional text: “a shimmering river floods these ruins.” Show reshaping and explain that unpainted areas are preserved.
6. **2:00–2:30 — Light:** paint vegetation or a ruin. Optional text: “golden light awakens these ruins.” Show the accumulated result.
7. **2:30–2:50 — Engineering:** explain local diffusion, regional masks, cumulative edits, and failure-safe state. State measured hardware/device and generation times accurately.
